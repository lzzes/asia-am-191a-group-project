# asia-am-191a-group-project
UCLA’s recent Time, Place, and Manner policies and enforcement have severely repressed students’ political expression. Our project aims to trace enforcement and repression through physical spaces on campus by reaching out to political organizations at UCLA and surveying the quality of various locations for demonstrations on a 1-5 scale for safety, empowering students’ organizing work on-campus. That said, it may be difficult to collect and visualize this data without putting these organizations at risk, so privacy and potential forms of anonymity will be key to consider.
